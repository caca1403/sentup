# Changelog

All notable changes to **Sentüp** are documented here. The format follows
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and the project uses
[Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [5.2]

### Added
- **Listening analytics** — a real donut chart (artist / song share), ranked
  bars and a 7-day activity chart, shown at the top of the Library and inside
  the Home feed. The donut legend and bars are interactive.
- **Single-flow Library** — the old tabs are replaced by one scroll with section
  headers, a quick jump-chip row (Karıştır · Analiz · Listeler · Sanatçılar ·
  Albümler · Şarkılar) and shuffle-all.
- **Keşfet channel picker** — channels are now selected from your followed
  channels with checkboxes instead of typing a comma-separated list.
- **Smart mixes that persist** — every smart-mix tile plays real tracks and can
  be kept as a real, saveable playlist; the fake empty tiles are gone.

### Changed
- YouTube home/metadata are requested in the app language (Turkish titles) and
  the parsers are locale-tolerant.
- Home greeting header is centred.

### Removed
- The broken Library sort capsule and its sort sheets.

## [5.1] and earlier
- Music + video in one app, rule-based Explore, downloads/offline, PiP, synced
  lyrics, visualiser, account switching and theming.
